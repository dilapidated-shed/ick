# Exact layers and normalized synthetic convolution

Accepted dependencies are MUL-1 d050bb1c66c4162ea3f4406f774317ef039dc9ed and
MUL-2 3c404d610292faa0a7234f4a7ba23cb2ae76de3e, both qualified with exact-head
CI. The shared STAR corpus and manuscript pins remain immutable. Machine
components use the qualified retained ICK compiler; independent references use
pinned Idriç Integer/Chez. No floating or whole-input oracle path is admitted.

The packed-product domain is a disk-grid Gaussian polynomial of power-of-two
length 2..8 at precision 2..256. Signed coefficients become carry-corrected
base-2^(4p) fields. A named conventional provider is permitted only for this
reduced model. It receives four pairs of unsigned evaluation magnitudes and
records identities and actual operand/result lengths. Signs are restored before
centered coefficient extraction. Padded high coefficients and final sign/carry
are checked, then real/imaginary combination, negacyclic folding and fg/r
normalization are explicit. Disk validation products are counted separately.

The desired type sketch distinguishes signed coefficient streams, packed
evaluation magnitudes, the four-product provider receipt, a centered extraction
certificate, ordered transform shapes, completed-layer grids, and exact
intermediate Gaussian dyadics. Capacity and aliasing are runtime contracts of
the pinned substrate; public failures preserve valid destinations.

Phase/layer admission must consume the actual accepted role/frame schedule.
The existing h=4 interface qualifies finite-support and Fourier-character
slices; it does not qualify a dense 2^64-entry array or the published recurrence.
Any additional reduced invariant representation must be derived and checked
against the same operators, including every auxiliary and spectator. Ordinary
butterflies cannot silently replace the requested motif path. Published calls
must refuse unmet row, precision, schedule and short-provider conditions.

The additional phase model is a closed quotient on four disjoint vectors
e_i tensor t_0 tensor t_0, each of weight nine. Their span is invariant under
all accepted Fourier-diagonal frame operators. Only X role zero may be
nonzero; every Y and auxiliary role is allowed. The primary tensor indicator
is the sum of the first three vectors. Source and sink endpoint characters
therefore stay in this span. Constant unused coordinates reduce C^tensor4
to the requested C^tensorD. Direct 16-character encoding and decoding change
representation; the complete accepted three-stage motif executes between them.
Routing, the inverse Y sign, i^27 endpoint phase, S_D and b^D=(1-i)^D/2^D
produce H0^tensorD. Only the completed layer is truncated toward zero.

The model reserves all 944 roles, including zero padding. It makes 16 full
frame calls per layer. Each invocation has an unchanged source bank; half
edges can add at most one fractional bit per stage. Character encoding,
three stages and b^D thus require at most p+11 fractional bits. An invocation's
intermediate coefficient is bounded by 16 times its incoming bound; three
stages and decoding need less than 2^17 magnitude. The supplied 32 fractional
and 32 magnitude guards are conservative. These are model-specific bounds,
not the published Delta or the paper's factor-two row reservation claim.

The direct exact H0 oracle matches 5,208 completed-grid records at p=5,9,33,
D=1..4, including every basis direction, half-grid cases and independently
tagged nonzero values in all Y and auxiliary roles. Reference SHA256 is
c57ae1b5dd52bf22fa6235318c49b1cda96acd8d8e1bb21ffce9b53461206a3b.
The first version charged 1,608,515,584 matrix entries per plan. Frame matrices
are independent of character frequency; the current version computes each
once and evaluates all sixteen characters against it, charging 100,532,224
matrix entries. The complete execution schedule is retained. There is no
paper saving or tape complexity claim for this model. The inverse-child
primitive retains the explicit (-i) Z C Z factorization.

The synthetic descriptor admits r=2,4,8 and at most four axes of lengths r or
r/2, with at most 64 polynomial records. It explicitly reserves all persistent
buffer and layer records against the caller's budget. K=1 layout L interleaves
named address bits by bit position; the polynomial suffix stays contiguous.
The DIF schedule writes frequency bits into their reversed named slots B.
Each grouped layer batches independent planes into the admitted Y/auxiliary
roles, with the X roles zero, and executes the complete motif. Forward calls
return LB F-minus; opposite calls consume LB, undo each twiddle before its
layer, and restore L at the end. Every completed round has one Qp site.

The squared transform error certificate is 2 ell^2 2^(-2p). Convolution
executes two retained-order forward calls, four logged packed subproducts per
record, the opposite call and the exact outer M scale. Its squared error
certificate is 2 M^2 (3 ell+1)^2 2^(-2p). Operands are checked against radius
1/2 and that error is strictly below 1/2 before execution, preserving the disk.
The result is cyclic convolution in the axes, negacyclic in y, divided by rM.
The declared final numerator width includes the log2 M rescaling allowance.
Conventional short multiplication remains explicitly reduced-model-only.
